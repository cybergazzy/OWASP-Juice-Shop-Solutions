In this challenge, you just have to intercept a request to see your basket

Fistly, you have to turn on intercept in burpsuite and then click on basket in the home page ![Basket](screenshots/HomePage.png)

Once you've intercepted the request, you will notice an id number so we can change that and forward the request just like how i changed mine from 6 to 4 ![Request Intercepted](screenshots/BurpIntercept.png)

Once the request is forwarded, the challenge is solved ![Done](screenshots/ScoreBoard.png)
